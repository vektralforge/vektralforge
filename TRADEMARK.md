# Trademark Policy

## Summary

The **code** is free. The **name and logo** are not, and that difference is
deliberate.

Apache 2.0 gives you extensive rights over the source code — use it, modify it,
redistribute it, build a commercial product on it. It explicitly does **not**
grant rights to the project's trademarks (see §6 of the licence). Keeping the
mark controlled is what prevents a modified or malicious build from presenting
itself as official VektralForge, which protects users rather than restricting
them. Linux, Kubernetes and Grafana all work this way.

## What you may do without asking

- State truthfully that your product, service or article uses, supports,
  integrates with, or is built on VektralForge.
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, courses, books and
  comparisons.
- Use the logo to link to the project or to illustrate a reference to it.
- Distribute unmodified builds under the name VektralForge.
- Say that you offer consulting, hosting or support **for** VektralForge.
- Use the name in a user group or meetup title, provided it does not suggest
  official status.

Nominative use — using the name to talk about the thing it names — does not
require permission and never will.

## What requires written permission

- Using the name or logo in **your** product name, company name, domain name or
  app name. `VektralForge Cloud`, `vektralforge-hosting.com` and
  `VektralForge Pro` all require permission.
- Distributing a **modified** build under the VektralForge name.
- Any use that implies endorsement, affiliation, certification or partnership.
- Merchandise bearing the mark.
- Registering the mark, or anything confusingly similar, in any jurisdiction.

Requests go to `trademark@vektralforge.org`. We aim to answer within fourteen
days and, in practice, say yes to most community uses.

## Forks

**You may fork VektralForge. That right is guaranteed by the licence and we will
never contest it.**

A fork must, however, use a different name. Not `VektralForge Community`, not
`VektralForge-NG`, not `VektralForj` — a genuinely distinct name. You may and
should state factually that your project is a fork of VektralForge, in text such
as *"MyProject, a fork of VektralForge"*. Remove the logo from the forked
project's branding, and keep the `NOTICE` file as Apache 2.0 requires.

This is not a restriction on your freedom to fork; it is the requirement that a
fork be identifiable as such, so users know what they are running.

## Usage guidelines

- Write the name as **VektralForge** — one word, capital V and F. Not
  `Vektral Forge`, not `VEKTRALFORGE`, not `Vektralforge`.
- Do not alter the logo's proportions, colours or orientation, and do not
  combine it with other marks to form a new one.
- Respect the clear space and minimum size defined in the brand manual at
  `docs/marca.md`.
- Do not use the mark in a way that suggests the project endorses a political
  position, a company or a product.

## Reporting misuse

Report suspected misuse to `trademark@vektralforge.org`. We prefer to resolve
these conversationally. Enforcement exists to prevent user confusion, not to
police the community.

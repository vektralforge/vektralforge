VektralForge. Roles, thresholds and the process for joining or leaving are
defined in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

Affiliation is declared for every person, including those who are independent.
This is not bureaucracy: it is what allows anyone to verify the composition rule
in `GOVERNANCE.md` §1.3 without having to research where people work.
Maintainers must report a change of affiliation within thirty days.

## How to join

Maintainership is earned through sustained contribution, not requested. Any
maintainer may nominate a contributor; the TSC approves by simple majority. See
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) §1.2.

Employment by ALEPH SERVER LTDA. is neither a requirement nor an advantage. As
the project grows, the TSC actively seeks maintainers from outside the sponsor
in order to strengthen — not merely satisfy — the composition rule.

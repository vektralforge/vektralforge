# Sponsors

VektralForge is sponsored by organisations that fund its development. Sponsorship
buys visibility and the project's gratitude. **It does not buy influence over
technical decisions, roadmap priority, or governance.**

## Founding Sponsor

### ALEPH SERVER LTDA.

[alephserver.cl](https://alephserver.cl)

ALEPH SERVER LTDA. created VektralForge and continues to sponsor it. The company
is an IT infrastructure consultancy based in Chile, and uses the project as the
foundation of its data engineering practice.

**What the sponsorship provides**

- Engineering time from contributed maintainers
- Infrastructure and hosting costs
- Custody of the trademark, licensed to the project and transferable to it or to
  a neutral foundation on request — see [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md)

**What it explicitly does not provide**

- No veto over technical decisions
- No casting vote in the Technical Steering Committee
- No reserved seat on the TSC — the 50% composition rule in
  [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) §1.3 applies to sponsor-affiliated members
  exactly as it does to everyone else
- No control over the licence — see
  [OPEN_SOURCE_PROMISE.md](OPEN_SOURCE_PROMISE.md)
- No priority on issues or pull requests

As of this writing, one of three TSC members is affiliated with ALEPH SERVER
LTDA. The current composition is recorded in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Becoming a sponsor

We welcome additional sponsors, and a more diverse funding base makes the project
more resilient. Write to `opensource@vektralforge.org`.

Sponsors are listed here with their name, logo and a link. Sponsorship is
disclosed publicly; we do not accept anonymous or undisclosed funding.

Sponsorship confers no rights beyond acknowledgement. Any sponsor asking for
influence over the roadmap in exchange for funding will be declined, and we would
rather say that here than in a negotiation.

## Individual support

Individual contributions are handled through the options in
[.github/FUNDING.yml](.github/FUNDING.yml). Funds cover infrastructure, domain
registration and, where possible, work by maintainers who are not employed by a
sponsor.
